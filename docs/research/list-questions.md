Semi-structured Interview Guide
1.	Introduction
This interview guide was developed to explore teachers' experiences of managing online English speaking classes for primary EFL learners. The questions were organised around the research questions of the study. Additional probing questions were used when necessary to clarify participants' responses or explore emerging issues in greater depth.
Section A. Background Information
1.	Could you briefly introduce yourself and describe your current teaching position? 
2.	How many years have you been teaching English? 
3.	How long have you been teaching online English classes? 
4.	Which grades or age groups do you currently teach? 
5.	Which online platforms do you usually use for your English speaking lessons?
Section B. Classroom Management Strategies (RQ1)
6.	Could you describe how you usually manage an online English speaking lesson from the beginning to the end?
7.	How do you establish classroom rules and routines in your online speaking classes?
8.	How do you manage turn-taking during speaking activities?
9.	What strategies do you use to maintain learners' attention and engagement throughout the lesson? 
10.	How do you support learners when they have difficulty speaking English?
11.	How do you use digital tools such as the chat box, breakout rooms, reaction icons, screen sharing, or digital whiteboards during speaking lessons?
Section C. Teachers' Perceptions (RQ2)
12.	In your opinion, what role does classroom management play in promoting speaking participation among primary learners?
13.	Which classroom management strategies do you consider most effective? Why?
14.	How do these strategies influence learners' confidence and willingness to communicate?
15.	Do different learners respond differently to the same classroom management strategies? Could you explain?
16.	Have your views about classroom management changed since you began teaching online? If yes, how?
Section D. Challenges (RQ3)
17.	What challenges do you most frequently encounter when managing online English speaking classes? 
18.	Which challenges have the greatest impact on learners' speaking participation?
19.	How do you usually deal with learners who are reluctant to participate in speaking activities? 
20.	How do you deal with technical problems that occur during online speaking lessons? 
21.	Are there any classroom management challenges that remain difficult to address? Please explain. 
Closing Question
22.	Is there anything else you would like to share about your experiences of managing online English speaking classes for primary EFL learners?
